I am standing as an independent candidate in St Brelade because I believe Jersey's future depends on the choices we make today about our environment and climate. My key commitments: 1. NET ZERO BY 2040 — Jersey must lead, not follow. I will push for binding legislation to achieve net zero emissions by 2040, five years ahead of current targets. 2. OFFSHORE WIND — I propose a £50 million investment programme in offshore wind capacity, creating hundreds of skilled jobs while securing Jersey's energy independence. 3. ELECTRIC TRANSPORT — Working with LibertyBus to convert the entire fleet to electric vehicles by 2032. Expanding the cycle network with 30km of protected lanes. 4. COASTAL PROTECTION — Establishing a dedicated Coastal Protection Fund of £20 million to address erosion and flooding risks in St Brelade, St Ouen, and Grouville bays. 5. GREEN SKILLS — A training programme to prepare 2,000 workers for careers in renewable energy, sustainable construction, and environmental management by 2030. 6. PLANNING REFORM — Stricter controls on greenfield development. Brownfield-first policy for all new housing. Mandatory green roofs on commercial buildings over 500sqm. I am not affiliated with any political party. I answer only to the people of St Brelade and to the evidence.
