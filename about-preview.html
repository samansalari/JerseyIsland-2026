About VotePulse

What it is

VotePulse is an independent, open-source election intelligence platform built for Jersey's 2026 general election. It exists to help voters understand where candidates stand on the issues that matter — housing, healthcare, tax, education, the environment and more.

VotePulse is not affiliated with any political party, candidate, or government body. It does not accept donations from political organisations and does not endorse or oppose any candidate.

How it works

1

Data collection

Candidate manifestos, public statements and news coverage are collected from publicly available sources — primarily flow.je, vote.je, policy.je and Jersey news outlets. Every piece of source data is stored with its original URL so nothing is lost.

2

AI analysis

AI (xAI Grok) reads each manifesto to generate plain-language summaries and extract policy positions mapped to specific issues. Each extraction includes the exact source quote from the original text and a confidence score.

3

Labelling & verification

All AI-generated content is clearly labelled with a warning badge. Users can expand any summary to read the original manifesto text and verify claims against the source quote.

4

Continuous updates

Data is refreshed every 6 hours. When a candidate's manifesto or public statements change, the system detects the difference (via SHA-256 hash), re-ingests the content, and re-runs the analysis. Previous versions are preserved in an append-only snapshot history.

Trust & transparency

  • !AI summaries may contain errors or misrepresentations. They are starting points for understanding, not definitive records.
  • !Always verify with the original text. Every AI summary links to the full manifesto and the specific quote that supports each claim.
  • !Confidence scores are shown for every extracted position (high, medium, low) so you know how certain the AI was about its reading.
  • !We do not editorially rank, endorse, or oppose any candidate. Candidates are listed alphabetically. Issue comparisons show raw data, not editorial judgement.

Data sources

There is no official API for Jersey election data. VotePulse collects information from publicly accessible web pages:

  • flow.je

    Candidate manifestos and profiles

  • vote.je

    Voter registration and election information

  • policy.je

    Policy documents and position papers

  • Local news outlets

    BBC Jersey, Bailiwick Express, ITV Channel, JEP (via RSS)

If a source is unavailable or changes format, VotePulse logs the failure in its ingest history so gaps are visible rather than silent.
